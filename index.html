Transformational Journey

How I grew

The journey and experiences that became turning points in the way I think, lead, and contribute.

Where it all started

I started out as a fairly active statistics student — with several technical projects, organizational roles, and internship experiences under my belt. But I was still working with an output-oriented mindset, not an impact-oriented one. I hadn't truly understood what it means to lead with clear values and vision.

Key shifts in thinking & action

I learned that leadership isn't about titles or achievements you can put on a CV — it's about consistency of values and the courage to serve. I began learning to build teams not just to complete tasks, but to grow together and create lasting impact.

Values & principles that shaped me most

Integrity in work, data-driven and empathetic leadership, and a commitment to continuous learning and giving. The principle of "impact before credit" guides me in every project and collaboration — that the best contributions don't need to be seen to remain meaningful.
